06 / Documentation

Every document
your port asks for.

Customs in Apapa rejects shipments for issues that customs in Mombasa wave through, and vice-versa. We know the difference.

B/L

Bill of Lading

Original 3+ copies. Received-for-shipment or on-board variants. Clean B/L on all dispatches.

CO

Certificate of Origin

DGFT-issued. India-origin certified. SAARC, GSP variants available where applicable.

CI

Commercial Invoice

USD or EUR. HSN codes, line-item breakdown, freight + insurance split per Incoterm.

PL

Packing List

Per-carton breakdown, NW/GW, CBM, item count, batch numbers, factory traceability.

SC

SONCAP

Nigeria-bound: PC, COC and Form M co-ordination with SON-appointed agents.

KE

KEBS PVoC

Kenya-bound: Pre-Verification of Conformity with Intertek / SGS / Bureau Veritas.

SA

SABS / NRCS

South Africa: regulator-compliant testing reports, letters of authority on regulated SKUs.

FC

Fumigation Cert

ISPM-15 compliant. Methyl bromide or heat-treatment, per destination country protocol.
