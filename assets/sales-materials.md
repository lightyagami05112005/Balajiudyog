# Balaji Udhyog — Sales Materials

> Slide-by-slide structures for the decks an export house actually uses. Design = the navy/gold/paper
> system, one real photograph per slide, minimal text, numbers over adjectives. Build in
> PowerPoint/Keynote/Slides or render from the brand HTML templates. **Old-money industrial, not
> start-up pitch.** No "vision/mission/disruption" fluff — capability and proof.

Shared design rules: 16:9 · navy background or warm-paper · one idea per slide · a single realism-passed
image per slide · Montserrat headings, Inter body · footer carries `balajiudhyog.com · export@…`.

---

## 1. Company Profile Deck (10–12 slides) — leave-behind / first meeting

1. **Cover** — logo mark · "Indian Hardware & Decor Exporters · Est. 1998" · hero foundry/port photo.
2. **Who we are** — §7.1 company intro (3 lines) · proof strip (25 yrs · 3,000+ containers · 14 markets).
3. **What we export** — the 7 categories as a clean grid (one product still each).
4. **Where it's made** — Muradabad / Aligarh / Firozabad, one aerial each + one line.
5. **How we work** — the six-stage process (Inquiry → Delivery) as a single horizontal flow.
6. **Quality & compliance** — QC stages + the conformity matrix (SONCAP/KEBS/SABS/GSA/TBS).
7. **Packaging built for ocean transit** — 4-stage protocol · <0.4% damage.
8. **Logistics** — lane map (Mundra → Apapa/Mombasa/Durban/Tema/Dar) · transit times · FOB/CIF.
9. **Why importers choose us** — the four trust points (direct / audit / paperwork / 35-day haul).
10. **Track record** — metrics + one quiet client-tenure line (19 years, Lagos).
11. **Terms at a glance** — MOQ bands, volume discounts, 90-day rate lock, private label from 500.
12. **Talk to the Africa desk** — named contact, WhatsApp, email, catalogue QR.

## 2. Distributor / Exclusive-Territory Pitch Deck (8–10 slides)

1. **Cover** — "Distribution partnership · [Country]".
2. **The opportunity in [Country]** — demand, ports, why-India-now (2–3 numbers about the market).
3. **What we bring** — direct factory access, consistency, smaller MOQs, mixed containers, heritage look.
4. **The partnership model** — exclusive territory on agreed volumes · 90-day rate locks · priority slots.
5. **Private label** — your house brand from 500 units · custom packaging · IP protection.
6. **De-risked start** — "one mixed container, approve samples first, factory named on PO".
7. **Marketing support** — line sheets, catalogue, finish samples, product photography.
8. **Proof** — track record + comparable-market reference (under NDA).
9. **Commercials** — indicative margins, volume bands, terms (T/T, L/C).
10. **Next step** — "first proforma against your top 20 SKUs" + signature/contact.

## 3. Buyer Presentation Flow (live call / screen-share, ~15 min)

```
1. Their world first  → "Tell me your market, ports, categories, seasons." (listen)
2. Relevance          → one slide on their country lane + compliance scheme
3. The fit            → 2–3 categories matched to their retail/project need
4. Proof              → QC + documentation + on-time/damage numbers (remove fear)
5. The sample promise → "approve the sample before we cut the order"
6. Container math     → carton/40' figures + indicative FOB→CIF for their port
7. Terms              → MOQ, 90-day lock, FOB/CIF, T/T or L/C
8. Close              → "shall I draft a proforma on your shortlist?"
```
Rule: 70% listening, 30% presenting. Every slide answers a buyer fear from `brand-positioning.md §4`.

## 4. Buyer presentation assets (the reusable kit)

- **Proof strip graphic** — 25 yrs · 600+ factories · <0.4% damage · 99.4% on-time · 14 markets.
- **Conformity matrix** — country × scheme (SONCAP/KEBS/SABS/GSA/TBS) × "handled before dispatch".
- **Lane map** — Mundra → African ports with transit times.
- **Six-stage process** strip.
- **Volume-band table** — Sample → Trade → LCL → FCL 20' → FCL 40' → Contract, with discounts + lead times.
- **Category one-pagers** — per category: top SKUs, MOQ, finishes, lead time, one product still.
- **Container-load reckoner** — pcs per 20'/40' per category (from the product spec sheets).
- **Sample dispatch card** — what we courier, timeline, cost handling.

All assets pull from existing, consistent sources: product spec sheets (`export-document-system/`),
the catalogue, and the realism-passed imagery — so every deck looks like one house.
