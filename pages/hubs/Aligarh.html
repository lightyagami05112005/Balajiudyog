/ The hub

India's lock capital — since 1870.

Aligarh has been making locks since 1870. Today, fifty kilometres north of Agra, it produces more than 75% of India's annual lock exports — concentrated in a 12 km radius of foundries, key-cutters, precision-engineering units and electroplating shops.

We source direct from twelve BIS-certified factories in this radius. Padlocks, mortise locks, cylinder locks, door closers, smart locks — all categories, all certifications: BIS, ISI, CE, ANSI Grade 2 where required.

Locks are the most regulated category in our catalogue. Aligarh's factories run testing labs on-site — 7-pin pick resistance, anti-saw bolt fatigue, salt-spray corrosion — and our docs team certifies output against destination-port standards before dispatch.

Aligarh's lock industry is older than the German automobile. And it still operates as a town.

/ Process

Six-stage
production.

Every piece moves through the same six stages — from raw material to container loading. Each stage QC-checked, each handoff documented.

01

Casting

Brass and zinc body castings produced in-foundry. Sand and die-cast methods used.

02

Machining

CNC machining of cylinders, bolts and internal mechanism. Precision tolerances.

03

Assembly

Hand assembly of pin tumbler mechanisms. Key-cutting matched to lock body.

04

Plating

Electroplating: nickel, chrome, brass-finish. PVD coatings on premium ranges.

05

Testing

7-pin pick resistance, anti-saw bolt cycle, salt-spray corrosion testing.

06

Packaging

Individual blister or branded boxes. Master cartons. Anti-rust desiccants.
