02 / Factory audits

We audit the floor,
not just the sample.

Every partner unit is assessed before it joins the network and re-checked on a cycle. You can name the factory in your PO, request its audit summary, and visit it.

Capability & capacity

Machinery, monthly output, and ability to hold tolerance at volume — verified on site.

Material & finish

Alloy, net weight and finishing process checked against the agreed specification sheet.

Social compliance

Labour and workplace standards (SA 8000 principles) assessed; documented and re-audited.

Twice-yearly floor walk

Our QC team walks every partner foundry's floor at least twice a year — audit, sampling, relationship.

Named accountability

One manager owns your order from inquiry to delivery — and signs off the factory it runs in.

Production transparency

Weekly production photos and timeline updates available on every order on request.

03 / Quality control

Six stages, every order.
You approve the sample first.

Quality is a procedure here, not a promise. Sampling and pre-shipment inspection are standard on every order over 1 CBM, with third-party QC available on request.

01

Sampling

Physical sample couriered and approved. Spec locked to that sample.

02

Incoming QC

Raw material, alloy and components checked before production.

03

In-line

Checks at casting, finishing and assembly — defects caught early.

04

Finish & weight

Finish, net weight and dimensional tolerance verified against spec.

05

Pre-shipment

Batch-sampled inspection; report issued. Third-party optional.

06

Load check

Carton count, marks and condition verified at container loading.
