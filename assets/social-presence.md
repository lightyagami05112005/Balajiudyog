# Balaji Udhyog — Business Social Presence

> A restrained, B2B social presence for an established export house. The goal is **credibility and
> reach to African importers**, not consumer engagement or follower counts. Show the operation: the
> floor, the containers, the documentation, the lane. Old-money industrial, never influencer.

**Channels, by priority:** LinkedIn (authority + distributor outreach) → WhatsApp Business (the actual
sales channel) → Instagram (visual proof / catalogue). No TikTok, no trend-chasing.

---

## 1. LinkedIn strategy (primary B2B channel)

**Page:** "Balaji Udhyog — Indian Hardware & Decor Exporters". Banner = a real container-loading or
foundry photograph. About section uses the §7 company introduction from `brand-positioning.md`.

**Content pillars (2–3 posts/week):**
1. **Proof of operation** — container loading at Mundra, foundry/QC floor, packing line. One photo,
   two lines of context. "40' FCL of brassware loaded for Apapa this week. 28-day production, clean
   SONCAP documentation."
2. **Market insight** — short takes on Africa import trends, freight on key lanes, compliance changes
   (links to the content articles).
3. **Hub stories** — Muradabad/Aligarh/Firozabad craft + scale.
4. **Trade education** — "FCL vs LCL for African importers", "what SONCAP actually requires".
5. **Milestones, quietly** — "19 years supplying our Lagos partner."

**Outreach use:** the page is the credibility backstop for cold distributor outreach (§ outreach-system).
Connect with importers, freight forwarders, sourcing managers; engage their posts before pitching.

**Tone:** informative, specific, zero hashtags spam (1–2 relevant tags max), no engagement-bait.

---

## 2. Instagram strategy (visual proof / catalogue)

**Handle:** @balajiudhyog. Bio: "Indian hardware & decor exporters · est. 1998 · Africa trade desk ·
catalogue on WhatsApp →".

**Grid as a living catalogue:**
- Product stills (the realism-graded images), one finish family per carousel.
- Behind-the-scenes: foundry, polishing, QC, packing, container loading (documentary, not staged).
- Hub features and shipment milestones.
- Saved Highlights: **Brassware · Bathroom · Locks · Glassware · Export Process · Markets**.

**Cadence:** 3–4 posts/week, 1 reel/week (a 15–20s documentary clip of the floor or a container
loading). Caption ends with "Catalogue & FOB pricing on WhatsApp — link in bio."

**Rule:** the feed must look like a real catalogue/operation, never like AI mood-boards. Use only
realism-passed imagery.

## 3. WhatsApp Business setup (the sales channel)

- **Verified WhatsApp Business** profile: logo, "Exporters · est. 1998", address, email, catalogue link,
  business hours (state IST + note Africa coverage).
- **Catalogue feature:** load the master + per-category collections as the WhatsApp catalogue so buyers
  browse in-app and tap to inquire.
- **Quick replies** (saved): MOQ note, FOB/CIF explainer, sample dispatch, document scheme, lead time.
- **Labels** for lead pipeline: New · Quoted · Sample sent · PO · Repeat · Cold. (Mirror the backend
  lead statuses.)
- **Greeting message:** "Balaji Udhyog — Africa export desk. Share your country, port and product list;
  we reply with samples and FOB pricing within one business day."
- **Away message** with response-time expectation across timezones.
- Wire the site's WhatsApp buttons to `wa.me/<number>?text=<prefilled>` using the templates in
  `export-copy-library.md`.

---

## 4. Africa buyer outreach flow (social → desk)

```
LinkedIn/IG content (proof) → profile visit → DM or WhatsApp inquiry
  → Africa desk replies <1 business day (catalogue + indicative FOB)
  → sample dispatch → proforma → first mixed container → repeat / distribution deal
```

- Use LinkedIn to **start** distributor conversations; move to **WhatsApp** to close.
- Tag every inbound social lead into the backend (`/backend`) with source = linkedin / instagram / whatsapp.

---

## 5. Posting principles (the guardrails)

- Show the operation, not slogans. Real floors, real containers, real documents (redacted).
- One idea per post. Specific over clever.
- No buzzwords, no hashtag stuffing, no giveaways, no follower-chasing.
- Francophone variants for West-African reach where it helps.
- Consistency of look = the realism-graded image system (`visual-direction.md`).
