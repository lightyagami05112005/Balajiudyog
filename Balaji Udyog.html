04 / Capabilities

Why importers across
the world work with us.

Sourcing from India is easy. Sourcing the right factory, at the right price, with documentation that clears your port — that's what we do. Direct factory partnerships, zero compromises.

/ 01

Direct factory sourcing

No middlemen, no markups. We work straight with verified manufacturing partners across India's craft hubs.

/ 02

Export quality assurance

Pre-shipment inspection, batch sampling, and third-party QC available on every order over 1 CBM.

/ 03

Competitive pricing

Volume contracts, FOB or CIF, with transparent BOM breakdowns and locked-in rates for 90 days.

/ 04

Bulk order support

FCL and LCL handling. Mixed-container consolidation across categories from a single PO.

/ 05

Reliable logistics

Direct shipping out of Mundra and Nhava Sheva. Dedicated forwarders for Lagos, Mombasa, Durban.

/ 06

OEM & private label

Build your house brand. Custom packaging, MOQ from 500 units, full IP protection on designs.

/ 07

Global market understanding

Two and a half decades shipping internationally. We know your duty structures, port behaviours, local distributor expectations, and the seasonal rhythms of your retail calendar.
