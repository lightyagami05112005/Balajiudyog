# Balaji Udhyog — Buyer Outreach System

> Templates and cadence for reaching African importers, distributors and chain-retail / project buyers.
> Tone: established export house writing to a fellow professional. Specific, brief, no marketing gloss,
> no buzzwords, no emoji in first contact. Every message ends with one clear, low-friction ask.

**Golden rules**
1. Lead with relevance to *their* market and category, not with us.
2. One ask per message. Make replying take ten seconds.
3. Specifics build trust: name the port, the lane, the MOQ, the document scheme.
4. Always offer the catalogue / a sample / indicative FOB — something concrete.
5. Reply within one business day, every time.

## 3. Distributor / exclusive-territory approach script (call or meeting)

1. **Open (relevance):** "We already ship [category] into [Port]; we're looking for one serious
   distribution partner in [Country/region]."
2. **Proof (60 seconds):** 25 years, 600+ audited factories, <0.4% damage, [scheme] handled, 99.4%
   on-time. Name a comparable market reference (under NDA).
3. **The offer:** exclusive territory on agreed volumes, 90-day rate locks, private-label option from
   500 units, marketing line sheets, priority production slots.
4. **De-risk:** "Start with one mixed container. Approve the samples first. We'll put the factory name
   on your PO."
5. **Ask:** "Shall we draft a first proforma against your top twenty SKUs?"

## 5. Follow-up cadence

| Day | Action | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | First touch (catalogue offer) | Email or WhatsApp |
| 2 | Light nudge ("did the catalogue reach you?") | same channel |
| 5 | Value add — a relevant insight (compliance note / lane cost), not a chase | Email |
| 9 | Offer a sample / a 15-min call with the Africa desk | WhatsApp |
| 16 | "Closing the loop" — leave the door open, set a seasonal reminder | Email |
| Seasonal | Re-quote ahead of their buying season; new freight lock | WhatsApp |

Stop at 4–5 touches if no engagement; move to a quarterly seasonal reminder. Never spam.

---

## 6. What never to do

- No mass identical blasts — segment by country + category.
- No buzzwords, no hype, no exclamation marks, no "limited time" pressure.
- No unrequested attachments in cold contact.
- No quoting a bare price — always the full line (MOQ, terms, container math, documents).
- No promising lead times the port won't keep.
