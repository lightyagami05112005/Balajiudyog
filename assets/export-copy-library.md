# Balaji Udhyog — Export Copy Library

> Buyer-facing copy for an Indian hardware & decor export house shipping to African markets.
> Voice: confident, specific, operator-grade. Never cheesy, never generic-corporate. Every line
> assumes the reader is a serious importer/distributor evaluating a sourcing partner.

**Voice rules**
- Lead with specifics (ports, MOQs, lead times, %s), not adjectives.
- Speak importer-to-exporter, not brand-to-consumer.
- Numbers build trust: 25 years, 600+ factories, 3,000 containers, 14 markets, <0.4% damage.
- Africa-first, never Africa-generic — name Lagos, Mombasa, Durban, Tema, Dar es Salaam.
- Avoid: "world-class", "one-stop solution", "we value our customers", "synergy", "best-in-class".

## 6. MOQ communication examples

- MOQ from **100 units** on cast brass & metal art; **500 units** on hardware lines.
- Don't have single-SKU volume? **Mix across categories** to reach a container — MOQ applies to the PO, not the piece.
- Sample orders **50–200 pieces** at list price; trade pricing kicks in from **200 units**.
- Volume bands: Sample → Trade (−5%) → LCL (−12%) → FCL 20' (−18%) → FCL 40' (−22%) → Contract (−26% + 90-day lock).
- "Tell us your shelf plan; we'll right-size the MOQ to your sell-through, not the other way around."

## 7. FOB / CIF inquiry templates

**Buyer → us (request)**
```
Company:        [name]            Country / Port: [e.g. Nigeria / Apapa]
Product / SKU:  [list]            Finish:         [option]
Quantity:       [units / CBM]     Inco term:      [FOB Mundra / CIF <port> / CFR]
Target price:   [optional]        Target delivery:[date / season]
Notes:          [private label, packaging, certs needed]
```

**Us → buyer (indicative quote skeleton)**
```
SKU:            [code] · [name]
Unit FOB Mundra:[USD x.xx]   |  Est. CIF [port]: [USD x.xx]
MOQ:            [units]       Finish: [options]
Carton:         [pcs/inner] · [pcs/master]
Container:      20' = [~pcs]  ·  40' = [~pcs]
Lead time:      [days] from PO + sample approval
Documents:      Commercial invoice · Packing list · B/L · CO · [SONCAP/KEBS/SABS]
Validity:       [30 days] · Volume contract: 90-day rate lock available
```

**Inco-term explainer (one line each, for product pages)**
- **FOB Mundra** — price to the rail of the vessel at Mundra; you arrange ocean freight & insurance.
- **CIF [your port]** — we cover cost, insurance and freight to your destination port.
- **CFR** — cost and freight to your port; insurance on your side.
