# Balaji Udhyog — Content Authority Library

Long-form, genuinely useful trade content written for African importers, distributors and sourcing
professionals. These articles back the Blog page and the country landing pages, establish authority on
India→Africa hardware export, and feed the LinkedIn/Instagram pillars. Every piece is practical (a buyer
can act on it), specific to real ports/schemes/lanes, and ends with a low-friction next step.

**House rules:** plain trade English · numbers over adjectives · no buzzwords · 1,000–1,800 words each ·
one clear takeaway · internal links to the relevant category + country landing page + an inquiry CTA.

## Publishing workflow
1. Draft here in markdown (single source of truth).
2. Render into the Blog template (`pages/Blog.html` article layout) keeping the navy/gold design.
3. Add `Article` JSON-LD (author, publisher, datePublished) per `seo-plan.md §5`.
4. Cross-link: each article → relevant category page + country landing page + inquiry CTA.
5. Repurpose the opening + one chart into a LinkedIn post (`social-presence.md`).
