/ FAQ

Questions we hear
before the first PO.

If something isn't here, send it through. We'll add it.

/ 01

What's your minimum order quantity?

MOQ varies by category — 50 pieces on India chandeliers, 200 on hand-cast brassware, 500–1,000 on mass-produced hardware. Mixed containers can combine smaller MOQs across categories.

/ 02

Payment terms for first orders?

30% advance against PI, 70% against scanned B/L copy before original release. LC at sight available after the third container.

/ 03

How long is your typical lead time?

21 days for in-stock hardware lines, 28–35 days for handcrafted brassware and metal artware, 45 days for custom chandeliers. Plus ocean transit.

/ 04

Do you do private labeling?

Yes — our OEM division has launched 40+ private-label lines for global distributors. MOQ from 500 units, custom packaging, IP protection on designs.

/ 05

Can you handle SONCAP / KEBS / SABS?

All major Global destination compliances are handled in-house by our docs team. Costs are itemised into your FOB. Inspection agents (Intertek, SGS, BV) are pre-paired per route.

/ 06

Do you ship LCL or only FCL?

Both. LCL consolidation team operates weekly out of Mundra. FCL preferred for orders over 18 CBM — usually cheaper per CBM by the time you factor in transhipment.
